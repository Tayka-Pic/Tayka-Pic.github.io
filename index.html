TAYKA

One photo prompt a day.
Everyone, everywhere, at once.

The same prompt lands on every phone in the world at the same instant. You get one photo to answer it.

  1. The prompt arrives

    Every day at the same moment, everywhere. Not a prompt chosen for you — the prompt, the one everyone else just got too.

  2. You take one photo

    Retake it as many times as you like. You still only post once, and the day is stamped by the server, so nobody gets two.

  3. Then the feed opens

    Not before. You can't see how anyone else answered until you've answered yourself — that's the whole app, and it's enforced by the database, not by a screen that politely asks you to wait.

Someone sent you here? They'll have included their handle — search it in the app once you're in, and their Tayks show up in your feed from the next prompt onward.
