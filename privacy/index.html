Tayka Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Tayka is a photo-a-day app: everyone in the world gets the same prompt at the same moment, and you can't see anyone else's answer until you post your own. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to reach us.

What we collect

  • Your identity, from Sign in with Apple. Tayka uses Sign in with Apple exclusively. There is no email or password account. Depending on the choice you made when signing in, we receive either your real email address or an Apple-generated private relay address, plus a stable identifier for your account. We never see your Apple password.
  • A username you choose. Shown next to everything you post.
  • The photos you post. One Tayk per day, tied to that day's prompt, plus up to two extra photos (ReTayks) on a day you have already answered.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Captions and comments you or others write on posts.
  • Your friends, your friend requests, and who you've blocked. Friendship on Tayka is mutual: you send a request, they accept, and only then do you see each other's Tayks. Blocking hides someone from you and you from them, in both directions, and removes any friendship or request between you.
  • Likes, comments and mentions. Likes are private: nobody but you can see what you liked, and the author is only told that somebody did. Comments are visible to whoever can see the Tayk they are on.
  • Hot Tayk votes. If you leave a post entered into Hot Tayk, other users may see it and vote on it. We record which vote you cast and which photos you were shown, so a fair win rate can be calculated.
  • Your streak. The number of days in a row you have posted. Your friends can see the number; nobody can see which days.
  • A notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications, so that Apple can deliver them. It is removed when you sign out.
  • The prompt ratings you give, if you rate a day's prompt. Ratings are private; only Tayka's operator sees the totals.

Advertising

Tayka shows at most three ads a day: one at the end of the feed, and, on a day with a long feed, one after every fifth Tayk. An ad is only ever shown on a day you have posted and after at least one friend's Tayk is on the page. Ads are what pay for the servers that store and deliver everyone's photos; that is the entire reason they are here.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. By default they are contextual: AdMob is told nothing about you, and the advertising identifier on your device is not used. In that mode nothing about you leaves the app for advertising purposes.

Once you have been using Tayka for a few days, iOS may ask whether you allow Tayka to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, or never answer, nothing changes: you keep getting the same contextual ads. If you say yes, your device's advertising identifier is shared with Google so the ads can be more relevant, which earns more and so covers the hosting bill sooner. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advanced personalization (a purchase)

Extra accent colors and app icons are a one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details. Apple tells us the transaction happened, and we record that transaction id against your account so the purchase can be restored on a new phone and a refund can be honored.

What we don't collect

We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we have no analytics SDK. There is no email/password login, so we never see or store a password. We don't collect precise location, we never read your address book or contacts list, and we do not ask for or store your phone number.

Where your data lives

Tayka's backend runs on Supabase, hosted in the US East region. Photos, posts, comments, and account data are all stored there, protected by row-level security rules that determine exactly who is allowed to read what. For example, a day's photos are invisible to you until you've posted your own that day, and blocked users cannot see each other's content regardless of who is friends with whom.

We also keep backups of the database and photos on hardware we control in the United States, so that an outage at our hosting provider cannot lose everyone's photos. Database backups are kept for 30 days and used for nothing but recovery, so data you delete may remain in a backup for up to 30 days after it disappears from the app. The copy of your photos is brought into line with the app within the hour, so a deleted photo leaves it the same day. Any copy of a backup that leaves that hardware is encrypted with a key that is not stored with it.

Companies that handle your data for us

  • Supabase for the database and photo storage, US East.
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, and push notifications.
  • Google AdMob for advertising, as described above.

Who can see what you post

Your photo and caption for a given day are visible to people who (a) are your friends and (b) have posted their own photo for that day. Comments on a post are visible to whoever can see that post. Four things reach further, each by your own choice:

  • Hot Tayk. Every new Tayk is entered into Hot Tayk unless you turn that off before posting. An entered Tayk can be shown the next day to other Tayka users who have posted, on the voting ballot and in Discover, and a winning Tayk is shown to everyone on Tayka.
  • Top Tayks. Up to four past Tayks you choose to feature on your profile are visible to anyone signed in to Tayka.
  • Your username and profile photo are visible to anyone signed in to Tayka, so that people can find you.
  • Reported content. If somebody reports a photo of yours, Tayka's operator can view that photo to review the report. Every such view is logged.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. This permanently removes your photos, posts, comments, follows, blocks, and account record. It cannot be undone.

Reporting content or contacting us

If you see something on Tayka that shouldn't be there, whether abusive content, harassment, or anything else, you can report it directly from the app (on a photo or a comment), or reach us by email. We review every report and respond within 48 hours.

Email: support@tayka.app

Blocking

Blocking someone in Tayka removes any friendship or request between you and that person, and hides their content from you and yours from them, including in Hot Tayk and Discover. You can review and undo your blocks at any time in Settings.

Children

Tayka is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
